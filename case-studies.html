Laser Systems OEM Industrial UV Laser Marking

UV Laser Window Optimization for 355nm Industrial System

Customer Application

UV laser equipment manufacturer operating 355nm solid-state laser systems for industrial marking applications.

Requirement

Output coupling window capable of sustained UV operation at 355nm without premature optical degradation, maintaining stable power output over extended production runs.

Engineering Challenge

Windows showed increasing absorption and color center formation within 1,000 hours, causing significant power drop. The standard fused silica windows with conventional AR coating were insufficient for the combined UV and power density requirements. Root cause analysis identified two issues: standard-grade fused silica with trace metal impurities causing solarization, and AR coating not optimized for high UV power density.

Material Selection

High-purity synthetic fused silica selected for low metallic impurity content, reducing solarization susceptibility under sustained 355nm exposure. Increased substrate thickness recommended for improved thermal management under high power density operation.

Coating Selection

UV-optimized AR coating designed for high power density at 355nm, with LIDT rated above 5 J/cm² at 355nm, 10ns pulse. Coating process adjusted to minimize absorption and defect density at DUV wavelength.

Specification Optimization

Surface quality upgraded from 40-20 to 10-5 to reduce scatter and absorption sites. Substrate thickness increased from standard for improved thermal dissipation under continuous UV exposure.

Manufacturing Route

Precision polishing to 10-5 surface quality followed by UV-optimized AR coating deposition using processes validated for DUV laser applications.

Inspection

Surface quality, dimensional accuracy, coating transmission and LIDT verified per project specification. Each component inspected for absorption defects and coating adhesion prior to shipment.

Final Specification

High-purity synthetic fused silica window with 10-5 surface quality, UV-optimized AR coating (LIDT >5 J/cm² at 355nm, 10ns), increased substrate thickness for thermal management. Component delivered per project specification with full inspection documentation.

Result

Optical lifetime extended significantly. Output power fluctuation substantially reduced over operating lifetime. Initial transmission measurably improved. Zero field failures reported in the first 12 months of deployment.

Documentation

Inspection report, material certification and coating performance data provided per project requirements.

Medical Device Manufacturer Diagnostic Imaging System

Precision Imaging Lens Assembly for Diagnostic Instrument

Customer Application

Medical diagnostic equipment manufacturer requiring a custom imaging lens for sensor-based diagnostic instruments operating in the visible spectrum (400–700nm).

Requirement

Custom 3-element imaging lens achieving diffraction-limited MTF across 400–700nm with less than 0.1% distortion. Tight centration tolerances required to maintain sensor image quality. Consistent performance across production batches.

Engineering Challenge

The customer’s initial single-vendor approach could not consistently meet MTF specifications across production batches. Achieving diffraction-limited performance with tight centration tolerances required coordinated optical design, precision manufacturing and rigorous incoming inspection.

Material Selection

BK7 and SF11 optical glass selected for the achromatic doublet plus correction element design. Material pairing chosen to correct chromatic aberration across the 400–700nm visible band while maintaining cost-effectiveness for medical device production volumes.

Coating Selection

Broadband AR coating optimized for 400–700nm visible range, applied to maximize transmission across all operational wavelengths of the diagnostic imaging system.

Specification Optimization

Through DFM review, center thickness tolerances on one element were identified as relaxable without affecting optical performance, reducing manufacturing cost. Cemented interface of the achromatic doublet optimized for minimum wavefront error. Centration specified to 30 arc-seconds.

Manufacturing Route

Precision polishing and centering of individual elements, cementing of achromatic doublet with optimized interface, and final assembly with interferometric verification of each element before assembly.

Inspection

Interferometric incoming inspection for each element before assembly. Final assembled-lens MTF verification across all fields and wavelengths. Distortion measurement confirmed compliance with specification.

Final Specification

3-element lens assembly (achromatic doublet + correction element) in BK7/SF11, centration to 30 arc-seconds, broadband visible AR coating. Delivered per project specification with full inspection documentation.

Result

MTF exceeded specification across all fields and wavelengths. Distortion measured well under 0.1% target. Batch-to-batch consistency maintained across multiple production runs. Significant cost reduction achieved through DFM tolerance optimization.

Documentation

Inspection report including interferometric data, MTF measurement results and material certification provided per project requirements.

Semiconductor Equipment OEM Wafer Inspection at 266nm

High-Purity Fused Silica Windows for Wafer Inspection System

Customer Application

Semiconductor equipment manufacturer developing a 266nm deep-UV wafer inspection system requiring optical windows with exceptional wavefront quality and extremely low particulate contamination.

Requirement

Optical windows with wavefront flatness of λ/20 at 266nm and extremely low particulate contamination levels. Consistent performance across multiple production batches.

Engineering Challenge

The initial supplier could not consistently meet flatness specifications and delivered components with unacceptable particle counts. The contamination and flatness requirements were critical to system signal-to-noise performance at 266nm.

Material Selection

Synthetic fused silica (Corning 7980 equivalent) selected for superior UV transmission and homogeneity at 266nm. Material grade chosen to minimize intrinsic absorption and wavefront distortion at DUV wavelengths.

Coating Selection

Coating selected based on application wavelength and transmission requirements at 266nm DUV operation.

Specification Optimization

Super-polish process implemented to achieve λ/20 flatness verified at 266nm. Surface quality and flatness specifications reviewed and optimized for manufacturability while maintaining application performance requirements.

Manufacturing Route

Precision super-polishing to achieve λ/20 flatness at 266nm. All final cleaning and inspection performed in partner Class 100 cleanroom facilities. Custom double-container packaging with cleanroom-compatible materials maintained cleanliness through delivery.

Inspection

Wavefront flatness verified via interferometry at 266nm. Particle count inspection performed in Class 100 cleanroom environment. Each window shipped with an individual particle count report.

Final Specification

Synthetic fused silica window (Corning 7980 equivalent) with super-polished surface achieving λ/20 flatness at 266nm. Cleanroom processed and packaged. Delivered per project specification with full inspection documentation.

Result

Wavefront flatness exceeded specification. Zero out-of-spec particle counts across all delivered batches. Improved signal-to-noise ratio achieved versus previous windows. Consistent performance maintained across multiple production batches.

Documentation

Inspection report including interferometric flatness data, particle count report and material certification provided per project requirements.

Research Laboratory Ultrafast Pulse Compression

Custom CaF₂ Prism Pair for Femtosecond Laser System

Customer Application

University research lab specializing in ultrafast laser science, operating a Ti:Sapphire laser system at 800nm for pulse compression experiments.

Requirement

Matched CaF₂ prism pair for pulse compression with apex angle matching better than 1 arc-minute and exceptionally low wavefront distortion. Standard catalog prisms had too much angle variation between individual pieces.

Engineering Challenge

Achieving apex angle matching better than 1 arc-minute between two separate prisms required manufacturing both in the same batch using identical tooling and process parameters. Standard catalog prisms could not meet this level of matching.

Material Selection

CaF₂ (calcium fluoride) selected for its broad transmission range covering the Ti:Sapphire 800nm operating wavelength, low dispersion characteristics suitable for pulse compression, and compatibility with ultrafast laser applications.

Coating Selection

Broadband AR coating at 700–900nm optimized for P-polarization, matching the Ti:Sapphire system operating wavelength and polarization state.

Specification Optimization

Apex angle matching specification set to better than 1 arc-minute, with both prisms manufactured from the same CaF₂ boule using identical tooling to minimize differential error.

Manufacturing Route

Both prisms manufactured in the same batch using identical tooling and process parameters to ensure matching. Precision polishing followed by broadband AR coating deposition optimized for the 700–900nm band.

Inspection

Apex angle measurement via autocollimator for both prisms. Transmitted wavefront verified at 633nm. Matched pair delivered as a set with comparison data between the two prisms.

Final Specification

Matched CaF₂ prism pair with apex angle matching better than 1 arc-minute, broadband AR coating at 700–900nm (P-polarization optimized), verified transmitted wavefront at 633nm. Delivered per project specification with full inspection documentation.

Result

Apex angle matching achieved well within the 1 arc-minute target. Transmitted wavefront quality met specifications per prism. Pulse compression successfully achieved. A second identical pair was subsequently ordered for an expanded beam setup.

Documentation

Inspection report including autocollimator apex angle comparison data, transmitted wavefront measurements and material documentation provided per project requirements.

Laser Systems OEM 532nm High-Power CW Laser Marking

High-Power CW Laser Window for Industrial Marking

Customer Application

Industrial laser marking company operating 2kW CW Nd:YAG systems at 532nm for high-throughput production marking applications.

Requirement

Laser window capable of sustained operation under 2kW CW power at 532nm without thermal lensing or coating degradation, eliminating frequent replacement and maintaining consistent marking quality.

Engineering Challenge

Previous windows experienced thermal lensing and coating degradation after only 500 hours of operation, causing frequent replacement and inconsistent marking quality. The combination of high CW power density at 532nm required both low-absorption substrate material and durable coating design.

Material Selection

High-purity synthetic fused silica with ultra-low OH content selected to minimize absorption at 532nm. Low OH content reduces intrinsic thermal loading under continuous-wave green laser operation.

Coating Selection

Dual-side broadband AR coating optimized for 515–545nm band with LIDT rated above 40 J/cm². Coating designed specifically for high-power CW operation at 532nm to prevent thermal degradation.

Specification Optimization

Surface quality specified at 20-10 and flatness at λ/10 to minimize thermal distortion under continuous operation. Specifications reviewed and optimized for manufacturability while maintaining application performance under 2kW CW conditions.

Manufacturing Route

Precision polishing of ultra-low OH synthetic fused silica to 20-10 surface quality and λ/10 flatness, followed by dual-side broadband AR coating deposition optimized for high-power CW green laser operation.

Inspection

Surface quality, flatness, coating transmission and LIDT verified per project specification. Each window inspected for absorption defects and coating integrity prior to shipment.

Final Specification

High-purity synthetic fused silica window with ultra-low OH content, 20-10 surface quality, λ/10 flatness, dual-side broadband AR coating at 515–545nm (LIDT >40 J/cm²). Delivered per project specification with full inspection documentation.

Result

Operational lifetime extended significantly compared to previous windows. Power fluctuation dramatically reduced over lifetime. Zero coating degradation observed at 2kW CW operation. Annual replacement cost significantly reduced.

Documentation

Inspection report, material certification and coating performance data provided per project requirements.

Laser Systems OEM 1064nm Laser Cavity

Custom 1064nm High-Reflectivity Mirror for Laser Cavity

Customer Application

Laser manufacturer building pulsed Nd:YAG laser systems requiring high-reflectivity cavity mirrors for resonator applications at 1064nm.

Requirement

HR mirrors with reflectivity above 99.8% at 1064nm, damage threshold above 10 J/cm², and wavefront distortion below λ/20. Previous supplier could only achieve 99.5% reflectivity with inconsistent damage thresholds.

Engineering Challenge

Achieving simultaneously high reflectivity, high damage threshold and low wavefront distortion required coordinated substrate preparation, multi-layer dielectric coating design and Ion-Assisted Deposition process control. The previous supplier’s inconsistent results indicated process limitations in coating density and adhesion.

Material Selection

Premium fused silica substrates selected for low thermal expansion, high laser damage resistance and excellent surface finish capability at 1064nm.

Coating Selection

35-layer HfO₂/SiO₂ dielectric coating stack optimized for 1064nm at 0° AOI. HfO₂ selected as high-index material for its superior laser damage resistance compared to alternative metal oxides. Ion-Assisted Deposition (IAD) used for dense, stable films.

Specification Optimization

Substrate flatness specified at λ/20 with 20-10 surface quality to ensure wavefront distortion remained within budget after coating. Coating layer design optimized for maximum reflectivity at 1064nm while maintaining damage threshold above requirement.

Manufacturing Route

Premium fused silica substrates polished to λ/20 flatness with 20-10 surface quality. 35-layer HfO₂/SiO₂ dielectric coating deposited using Ion-Assisted Deposition (IAD) for dense, stable films with high laser damage resistance.

Inspection

100% LIDT testing at 10 J/cm². Reflectivity measured at 1064nm. Wavefront distortion verified via interferometry. Each mirror underwent full inspection prior to shipment.

Final Specification

Premium fused silica HR mirror with 35-layer HfO₂/SiO₂ dielectric coating, reflectivity above 99.8% at 1064nm, LIDT above 10 J/cm², λ/20 flatness, 20-10 surface quality. Delivered per project specification with full inspection documentation.

Result

Reflectivity exceeded 99.8% specification. LIDT exceeded damage threshold requirement. Wavefront distortion measured better than λ/20 specification. Batch consistency maintained across multiple production runs.

Documentation

Inspection report including reflectivity measurement, LIDT test results, interferometric wavefront data and material certification provided per project requirements.

Semiconductor Equipment OEM 193nm DUV Wafer Defect Detection

193nm DUV Inspection Lens Assembly for Wafer Defect Detection

Customer Application

Semiconductor equipment manufacturer developing a 193nm deep-UV wafer inspection system for defect detection in advanced semiconductor fabrication.

Requirement

Custom 4-element lens assembly using DUV-grade CaF₂ and fused silica with less than 50nm RMS wavefront error, above 95% transmission at 193nm, and resistance to solarization under prolonged DUV exposure.

Engineering Challenge

Combining DUV-grade materials (CaF₂ and fused silica) in a multi-element assembly while maintaining sub-50nm RMS wavefront error, high transmission at 193nm, and long-term solarization resistance. Assembly required precise centering in cleanroom conditions to meet optical performance targets.

Material Selection

DUV-grade synthetic fused silica and spectroscopic-grade CaF₂ sourced with verified above 99% transmission at 193nm. Material grades selected for minimum solarization susceptibility under prolonged deep-UV exposure.

Coating Selection

AR coatings optimized for the 190–200nm band to maximize transmission at the 193nm operating wavelength while maintaining coating durability under prolonged DUV exposure.

Specification Optimization

All elements polished to λ/20 flatness and 10-5 surface quality. Specifications reviewed and optimized for manufacturability while maintaining sub-50nm RMS wavefront error and above 95% assembly transmission requirements.

Manufacturing Route

Precision polishing of all elements to λ/20 flatness and 10-5 surface quality. DUV-optimized AR coating deposition. Assembly performed in partner Class 100 cleanroom facilities with precise centering to less than 10 arc-seconds.

Inspection

Wavefront error measured via interferometry. Assembly transmission verified at 193nm. Solarization resistance confirmed through extended DUV exposure testing. Centering alignment verified during assembly.

Final Specification

4-element lens assembly using DUV-grade synthetic fused silica and spectroscopic-grade CaF₂, with DUV-optimized AR coating (190–200nm), assembled in Class 100 cleanroom with centering below 10 arc-seconds. Delivered per project specification with full inspection documentation.

Result

Wavefront error achieved within the 50nm RMS specification. Assembly transmission exceeded 95% target at 193nm. No measurable solarization observed after extended DUV exposure testing. Defect detection resolution improved to advanced node capability.

Documentation

Inspection report including wavefront error measurement, transmission data at 193nm, solarization test results and material certification provided per project requirements.

Illustrative Engineering Example
Medical Device Manufacturer Endoscopic Imaging System

Biocompatible Sapphire Window for Endoscopic Imaging

Customer Application

Medical device manufacturer developing a reusable endoscope requiring a distal window that maintains optical clarity through repeated clinical use and sterilization cycles.

Requirement

Distal window capable of withstanding repeated autoclave sterilization (134°C, 2 bar), resistant to scratching from surgical tools, and maintaining optical clarity. Custom 3.5mm diameter with 0.5mm thickness. Previous BK7 and standard glass windows were failing due to surface damage after 20–30 sterilization cycles.

Engineering Challenge

Standard optical glasses (BK7) could not survive repeated autoclave sterilization cycles and were susceptible to surgical instrument scratching. The combination of extreme hardness requirement, biocompatibility, autoclave resistance and small custom dimensions presented a multi-constraint material and manufacturing challenge.

Material Selection

Optical-grade sapphire (Al₂O₃) selected as the ideal material — 9H hardness immune to surgical instrument scratching, material compatibility evaluated per ISO 10993 for biocompatibility, and inherently resistant to autoclave sterilization conditions (134°C, 2 bar).

Coating Selection

Broadband AR coating at 400–700nm applied to maintain optical clarity for visible imaging through the endoscope. Coating selected for adhesion and durability on sapphire substrate through repeated autoclave cycles.

Specification Optimization

Flatness specified at λ/4 for endoscopic imaging quality. Custom dimensions of 3.5mm diameter with 0.5mm thickness achieved through precision processing of sapphire. Specifications reviewed and optimized for the sapphire material characteristics.

Manufacturing Route

Sapphire crystal precision cut and polished to λ/4 flatness. Custom 3.5mm diameter with 0.5mm thickness achieved through specialized sapphire processing techniques. Broadband visible AR coating applied to both surfaces.

Inspection

Flatness, surface quality, transmission and coating adhesion verified per project specification. Scratch resistance and autoclave durability validated through accelerated lifecycle testing.

Final Specification

Optical-grade sapphire (Al₂O₃) window, 3.5mm diameter × 0.5mm thickness, λ/4 flatness, broadband AR coating (400–700nm), biocompatible per ISO 10993 evaluation. Delivered per project specification with full inspection documentation.

Result

Survived extensive autoclave cycling with zero surface degradation. No measurable transmission loss after sterilization testing. Scratch resistance confirmed — no damage from standard surgical tool contact. Material compatibility verified per ISO 10993 evaluation.

Documentation

Inspection report, material certification, biocompatibility evaluation documentation and sterilization cycle test results provided per project requirements.
