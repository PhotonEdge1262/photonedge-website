Optical Solutions by Industry

Every application has unique optical challenges. We provide end-to-end solutions — from material selection to custom coating — tailored to your industry's demands.

Laser System Optics

High-power laser systems demand optics with exceptional damage thresholds, precise reflectivity, and stable performance under thermal load. PhotonEdge provides laser-optimized mirrors, windows, lenses, and beamsplitters with coating solutions for CO₂, Nd:YAG, fiber, excimer, and ultrafast lasers.

Key Challenges We Solve:

  • Mirror coating degradation at high power → High-LIDT dielectric coatings
  • Thermal lensing in high-energy systems → Low-absorption substrate selection
  • Beam quality maintenance → Precision surface figure and roughness

Recommended Components:

  • Laser Line HR Mirrors
  • Broadband Dielectric Mirrors
  • UV Fused Silica Windows
  • UV Fused Silica Lenses
  • Cube Beamsplitters

Semiconductor & Metrology Optics

Wafer inspection, lithography support, and metrology systems require optics with extreme surface quality, sub-nanometer roughness, and exceptional cleanliness. PhotonEdge supplies precision windows, filters, and custom optics meeting semiconductor-grade requirements.

Key Challenges We Solve:

  • Particle contamination sensitivity → Class 100 cleanroom packaging
  • Sub-nm surface roughness requirements → MRF polishing capability
  • Narrow bandpass filtering for inspection → Custom interference filters

Recommended Components:

  • UV Fused Silica Windows
  • Narrow Band Filters
  • Achromatic Doublet Lenses
  • Aspherical Lenses
  • BK7 Precision Windows

Medical & Life Sciences Optics

Medical imaging, endoscopy, microscopy, and diagnostic equipment require biocompatible, high-transmission optics with consistent quality across production batches. PhotonEdge delivers medical-grade optical components with full traceability and regulatory support.

Key Challenges We Solve:

  • Batch-to-batch consistency → Statistical process control
  • Bio-compatibility requirements → Material certification and documentation
  • Miniaturized optics for endoscopes → Precision small-diameter manufacturing

Recommended Components:

  • Achromatic Doublet Lenses
  • Aspherical Lenses

  • Linear Polarizers
