Material Selection

Choosing the right optical material is critical to performance. We help you evaluate materials based on your wavelength range, operating environment, thermal requirements, mechanical constraints and budget.

Material Families We Work With:

  • BK7 / B270 — Visible/NIR general purpose, cost-effective
  • UV Fused Silica — UV to NIR, high laser damage threshold
  • CaF2 — Deep UV to mid-IR, low dispersion
  • Sapphire — UV to mid-IR, extreme hardness (9H)
  • ZnSe — Visible to far-IR, CO2 laser standard
  • Germanium (Ge) — Mid-IR to LWIR, high refractive index
  • Silicon (Si) — NIR to MWIR, excellent thermal conductivity

Optical Coatings

Optical coatings determine how light interacts with your component surfaces. We provide guidance on selecting the right coating type for your wavelength, angle of incidence, power level and environmental conditions.

Anti-Reflection (AR)

Single-layer and multi-layer AR coatings for specific wavelength ranges. Residual reflectivity below 0.1% at design wavelength.

High-Reflectance (HR)

Dielectric HR mirrors with reflectivity above 99.9% at design wavelength. Custom designs for laser cavities and beam steering.

Dichroic Filters

Precision wavelength-selective coatings for fluorescence, multi-spectral imaging and wavelength division applications.

Metallic Coatings

Protected Silver, Protected Gold, Enhanced Aluminum — broadband reflection from UV to far-IR with environmental protection.

Tolerance & DFM Review

"Tight tolerances are not always necessary. We review drawings to identify tolerances that matter and tolerances that may be unnecessarily expensive."

When you submit a drawing or specification, our engineering team reviews:

  • Are the tolerances achievable with standard manufacturing processes?
  • Can tighter tolerances be relaxed without affecting system performance?
  • Is the material compatible with the required coatings?
  • Are there edge thickness, chamfer, or mounting considerations?
  • Is the surface quality specification appropriate for the application?
  • Can design modifications reduce cost without sacrificing performance?

DFM review is included at no charge with every custom quote.

Packaging & Handling

Optical components require careful handling to maintain surface quality and coating integrity during transit.

  • Clean room handling and packaging to minimize particulate contamination
  • Individual protective wrapping for each component
  • Appropriate packaging materials for fragile optics (foam, dividers, rigid containers)
  • Moisture protection with desiccant for hygroscopic materials (CaF2, ZnSe)
  • Clear labeling with part number, specification reference and handling instructions
