Illustrative Engineering Example

High-Purity Fused Silica Windows for Wafer Inspection System

Semiconductor equipment OEM needed DUV windows with λ/20 flatness and ultra-low particulate contamination. Synthetic fused silica with super-polish and cleanroom processing delivered consistent results across batches.

Illustrative Engineering Example

Precision Imaging Lens Assembly for Diagnostic Instrument

Medical device manufacturer required diffraction-limited MTF across 400-700nm with <0.1% distortion. Achromatic doublet plus correction element with interferometric inspection achieved specification across production batches.

Illustrative Engineering Example

Custom CaF₂ Prism Pair for Femtosecond Laser System

Research lab needed matched CaF₂ prism pair for pulse compression at 800nm. Batch manufacturing with identical tooling achieved 15 arc-second apex angle matching and <30 fs pulse compression.
