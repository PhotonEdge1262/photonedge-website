Why PhotonEdge

We're not a catalog company. We're an engineering partner. Here's what that means in practice.

Engineering First

Every project starts with an engineering review. We tell you if your spec makes sense, what can be relaxed to save cost, and what actually needs to be tight for performance. We don't just quote whatever you send us.

Custom When Standard Doesn't Fit

Most systems don't fit perfectly into catalog specifications. When a standard part won't work, we design and manufacture custom components — from single prototypes to production volumes — without the premium you'd pay from a Western catalog house.

Specification Optimization

Tighter tolerances mean higher cost — but not always better system performance. We help you identify which specs really matter for your application, so you pay for precision where it counts and relax tolerances where it doesn't.

Global Supply Chain

Our partner network spans multiple factories across China and Southeast Asia, each specialized in different materials and processes. We match your project to the right facility, so you get appropriate quality at the right price point.

Manufacturing Partner Network

We work with a carefully selected network of manufacturing partners, each specializing in different optical materials, processes, and volume scales. We assign your project to the partner best matched to your specific requirements.

Precision Optics (South China)

Specializes in high-precision spherical and aspherical lenses, prisms, and windows. Advanced polishing and coating capabilities.

  • Materials: BK7, fused silica, CaF₂, sapphire
  • Surface quality: up to 10-5
  • Flatness: up to λ/20
  • Coatings: dielectric, metal, AR, HR

Infrared Optics (North China)

Focused on infrared materials and diamond turning. Germanium, silicon, ZnSe, and chalcogenide optics for thermal imaging.

  • Materials: Ge, Si, ZnSe, ZnS, chalcogenide
  • SPDT diamond turning for aspheres
  • DLC and IR AR coatings
  • MWIR and LWIR applications

Volume Production (East China)

High-volume manufacturing for consumer electronics, automotive, and industrial applications. Automated polishing and coating lines.

  • High-volume: 10k – 1M+ pcs/year
  • Automated inspection
  • ISO 9001 & IATF 16949
  • Cost-optimized processes

All manufacturing partners are pre-qualified with quality audits and sample verification. We regularly re-audit to maintain standards.
