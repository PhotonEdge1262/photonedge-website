Our Manufacturing Network

PhotonEdge works with a network of qualified manufacturing partners, each selected for specific capabilities. We match each project with the partner best suited to its requirements.

Precision Polishing Partners

Specialized in surface quality 10-5 and below, flatness λ/10 and better. For demanding optical surfaces requiring controlled figure and scratch-dig.

IR Material Specialists

Experienced with germanium, silicon, zinc selenide, zinc sulfide. For thermal imaging, FLIR, and IR laser applications.

Optical Coating Facilities

BBAR, HR, dichroic, metallic coatings with spectrophotometer verification. LIDT-tested for high-power laser applications.

Custom Geometry Capability

Aspherical surfaces, cylindrical optics, prisms, custom mounts. For non-standard geometries that require specialized tooling.

Inspection & Metrology

Interferometry, spectrophotometry, surface profilometry. For components requiring documented verification of all specifications.

Prototype to Volume

Partners capable of single-unit prototypes and partners optimized for volume production. We route your project accordingly.

One point of contact. You work with PhotonEdge. We coordinate the network — so you don't need to manage multiple suppliers.
