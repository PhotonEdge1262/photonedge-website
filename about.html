About PhotonEdge

PhotonEdge is not a manufacturer. PhotonEdge is an Optical Engineering & Supply Chain Company.

Our Philosophy

The optical industry doesn't need another manufacturer. It needs a reliable partner who understands the full chain — from material selection through coating design, manufacturing, inspection, and delivery — and takes responsibility for the result.

That's what PhotonEdge is. We combine engineering expertise with a carefully managed network of manufacturing partners to deliver optical components that meet your specifications, on schedule, with full documentation and traceability. We're not the biggest. We're not the cheapest. We're the partner who makes your optical supply chain work reliably.

Engineering First

Every conversation at PhotonEdge starts with engineering. When you contact us, you don't get a salesperson who reads from a catalog — you get someone who can discuss wavelength-dependent material properties, coating design tradeoffs, and tolerance implications for your specific application.

Our engineering team has deep expertise across the full optical spectrum — from deep UV systems at 193 nm to long-wave infrared thermal imaging at 12 μm. We understand the physics of how optical components perform under real operating conditions, not just how they look on a datasheet.

Supply Chain Strategy

No single factory excels at every optical process. Some specialize in precision polishing. Others in complex coatings. Others in IR materials. PhotonEdge maintains a curated network of Manufacturing Partners — each selected for specific capabilities, and each held to our quality standards.

This approach means we match your project to the optimal manufacturing capability for your specific requirements — rather than forcing every job through a single factory's limitations. You get components made by the best process for your application, managed through a single point of contact.

Quality Commitment

Quality at PhotonEdge means every component is inspected against your specifications before shipment. Not a sampling — every piece. Full inspection reports are included with every order, covering surface quality, flatness, centration, and coating performance.

Our quality system extends beyond final inspection. We manage quality through the entire chain: incoming material verification, in-process dimensional checks, coating process monitoring, and final inspection with calibrated equipment. This systematic approach is how we deliver consistent quality across our manufacturing network.

Global Vision

We serve optical engineers and procurement teams across the globe — from laser system integrators in Germany to semiconductor equipment companies in Japan, research labs in the US, and medical device manufacturers worldwide.

Our commitment is to make working with PhotonEdge as seamless as working with a local supplier. English-speaking engineering support, responsive communication, clear documentation, DDP/DAP shipping with full customs support, and the technical depth to be a genuine engineering partner — not just a vendor.

Optical Materials

K9 (BK7)

K9 (equivalent to BK7/SCHOTT) is the most widely used optical glass. Transmission range: 330nm-2100nm. Refractive index Nd=1.51637, Abbe number Vd=64.17. Excellent homogeneity and low bubble content. Standard choice for visible and near-IR applications including lenses, prisms, windows, and mirror substrates. Available with AR, HR, and beamsplitter coatings.

Quartz

UV Fused Silica (JGS1/JGS2/JGS3) offers exceptional transmission from 185nm to 2500nm with very low coefficient of thermal expansion (0.58×10⁻⁶/°C). Ideal for UV optics, high-power laser applications, and environments with large temperature fluctuations. Superior homogeneity and minimal fluorescence. Used in excimer laser systems, UV spectroscopy, and precision imaging.

ZnSe

Zinc Selenide (ZnSe) transmits from 600nm to 18µm with high IR transmission (T>70% @ 10.6µm). Chemically inert and non-hygroscopic. The preferred material for CO2 laser optics (10.6µm) and FLIR thermal imaging windows. Available with AR/DLC coatings for high-power and environmental protection. Minimum order quantities may apply due to material sourcing.

Ge

Germanium (Ge) transmits from 2µm to 14µm with high refractive index (Nd=4.003 @ 10.6µm), enabling compact optical designs. High hardness and good thermal conductivity. Widely used in LWIR thermal imaging, FTIR spectroscopy, and IR sensing systems. Requires anti-reflection coating due to high surface reflectivity (~36% per surface uncoated). Temperature-sensitive above 100°C.

Si

Silicon (Si) transmits from 1.2µm to 7µm and again from 25µm to 300µm (far IR). Low density (2.33 g/cm³) and high thermal conductivity make it ideal for lightweight IR optics and laser scanning mirrors. Cost-effective alternative to Ge in MWIR (3-5µm) systems. Widely used in IR cameras, gas sensors, and free-space optical communication.

CaF2

Calcium Fluoride (CaF2) transmits from 170nm to 7.8µm — one of the broadest transmission ranges of any optical material. Very low refractive index (1.4289 @ 587nm) enables high-efficiency AR coatings. Low solubility and high laser damage threshold make it ideal for UV excimer laser optics, fluorescence microscopy, and spectroscopy. Available in UV-grade (193nm ArF) and IR-grade variants.

Sapphire

Sapphire (Al₂O₃) transmits from 180nm to 4.5µm with extreme hardness (Mohs 9), making it virtually scratch-proof. Excellent chemical resistance and thermal stability up to 2000°C. Ideal for harsh environment windows, pressure viewport windows, watch crystals, and UV/VIS/IR windows requiring maximum durability. High birefringence must be considered in precision applications.

MgF2

Magnesium Fluoride (MgF2) transmits from 115nm to 6.5µm — one of the shortest UV cutoff wavelengths of any optical material. Low refractive index (1.376 @ 587nm) makes it the industry standard single-layer AR coating for visible optics. Widely used in VUV optics, excimer laser windows, and as a coating material for broadband AR applications.
