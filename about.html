An Experienced, Engineering-Led Optical Company

PhotonEdge is an optical company focused on precision optical components, custom optics and engineering support. We combine optical industry experience with flexible manufacturing and supply-chain resources to provide practical solutions for laser, research, imaging, semiconductor and industrial applications.

Understand the requirement. Select the right solution. Deliver the right optic.

Why PhotonEdge

PhotonEdge was created to bring our years of optical industry experience into a dedicated international brand. Before PhotonEdge, our business grew through domestic customers, online platforms and long-term relationships across the optical industry.

We built practical experience in products, applications, sourcing, manufacturing coordination and customer service. Today, PhotonEdge brings that experience to international customers.

We are not simply trying to sell optical components from a catalog. We aim to understand the application behind the specification and provide the appropriate product, manufacturing route and level of support.

PhotonEdge is the international brand of Beijing Hengdingguang Technology Co., Ltd.
