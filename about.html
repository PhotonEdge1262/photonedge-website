Our Manufacturing Model

PhotonEdge does not rely on a single factory for every optical process. Different optical processes require different equipment, expertise, and environments. No single facility excels at all of them.

We work with qualified manufacturing partners selected according to material, geometry, tolerance, coating, quantity and application requirements. Each partner is chosen for specific capabilities relevant to your project.

One engineering contact. Multiple manufacturing capabilities.

Why This Model Matters

Different projects demand different manufacturing strengths. A UV laser window requires different capabilities than an IR thermal imaging lens. A prototype CaF₂ prism pair needs a different approach than a volume production run of BK7 windows.

Quality & Documentation

Inspection documentation and material certificates are available according to project requirements. We work with our manufacturing partners to ensure components are inspected against your specifications before shipment.

Documentation available upon request includes inspection reports covering surface quality, dimensional accuracy, coating performance, and material traceability. Specific inspection methods and acceptance criteria are agreed during the engineering review phase.
