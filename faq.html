Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about ordering optical components from PhotonEdge.

Ordering & Pricing

What is your minimum order quantity?

For standard catalog components, there is no minimum order quantity — you can order a single piece. For custom manufactured optics, MOQ typically starts at 5-10 pieces depending on complexity.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank wire transfers (T/T), PayPal, and major credit cards. For established customers, net-30 payment terms may be available after credit approval.

Do you offer quantity discounts?

Yes, tiered pricing is available for volume orders. Contact our sales team for a customized quote based on your requirements.

Lead Time & Shipping

What is your typical lead time?

Standard components ship within 3-5 business days. Custom manufactured optics typically take 2-4 weeks depending on specifications and coating requirements. Rush orders may be available — please inquire.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship worldwide via DHL, FedEx, and UPS. Import duties and taxes are the responsibility of the customer.

Custom Optics & Technical

Can you manufacture optical components to my specifications?

Yes. We specialize in custom precision optics. Send us your drawings, specifications, or application requirements and we will review them and identify a suitable manufacturing approach.

What materials do you work with?

We work with BK7, UV Fused Silica, Sapphire, CaF2, MgF2, Germanium, Silicon, ZnSe, and other optical materials. Specialty materials can be sourced upon request.

What coating options are available?

We offer AR coatings (VIS, NIR, SWIR, MWIR, LWIR), HR coatings, partial reflectors, broadband coatings, laser line coatings, and custom coatings for specific wavelength ranges.

Can you help with material selection or specifications?

Our engineering team can provide technical support for material selection, coating recommendations, and manufacturability feedback. If you are not sure what you need, we can help identify the key specifications for your application.

Quality & Documentation

Do you provide inspection reports and documentation?

Inspection documentation and material documentation can be provided when required. This includes dimensional data, surface quality reports, and coating performance measurements. Certificates of Compliance are available with shipments.

What tolerances can you achieve?

Tolerances depend on the component type, material, and application. We work to the specifications agreed for each project. Contact us with your requirements and we will confirm what is achievable.

Support

Do you offer sample components?

We can provide sample components for qualification testing, typically at standard pricing with credit applied toward future volume orders.

What is your warranty policy?

All optical components come with a 12-month warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. Products must be stored and handled according to recommended guidelines.

Still Have Questions?

Send us your requirements and our team will get back to you within 24 hours.
