Learn About Our Engineering Approach

Design for Manufacturability Review

We help optimize specifications for performance, manufacturability and cost.

Before production begins, PhotonEdge reviews your design for manufacturing feasibility. Our goal is not to change your requirements — but to identify specifications that may unnecessarily increase cost or lead time, while maintaining the performance your application requires.

1

Unnecessary Tight Tolerances

Identifying tolerances tighter than your application actually requires, which increase manufacturing time and cost.

2

Material Alternatives

Suggesting equivalent or more cost-effective materials that meet the same optical and environmental requirements.

3

Coating Feasibility

Reviewing coating specifications for manufacturability and suggesting alternatives that achieve the same optical performance.

4

Manufacturing Process Selection

Recommending the most appropriate manufacturing route based on geometry, material, tolerance and quantity.

5

Dimensional Constraints

Checking that all dimensions can be achieved with standard or specialized tooling.

6

Inspection Requirements

Aligning inspection methods with actual specification requirements to avoid unnecessary testing cost.
