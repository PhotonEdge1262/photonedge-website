Custom Optical Solutions

We select the appropriate manufacturing route for each project.

Different optical processes require different manufacturing capabilities. We coordinate material selection, precision manufacturing, coating and inspection based on your specific requirements.

What We Coordinate

From material to finished optic, we manage the key stages of your custom optical component.

🧪

Material Selection

BK7, fused silica, CaF2, sapphire, ZnSe, germanium and other optical materials. We help select the right material for your wavelength and application.

📐

Precision Manufacturing

Lenses, windows, mirrors, prisms, filters and other optical components. Standard or custom geometries manufactured to your specifications.

🔬

Coating

Anti-reflection, high-reflectance, bandpass, dichroic and metallic coatings. Optimized for your wavelength and power requirements.

✅

Inspection & Documentation

Dimensional, surface and optical inspection according to agreed specifications. Inspection reports and material documentation provided when required.

Flexible Manufacturing Resources

We are not a single factory. We work with a network of manufacturing partners and select the most appropriate production route for each project based on material, geometry, tolerance, coating and quantity.

This approach means your project is matched to the manufacturing capability best suited to its requirements, rather than being forced into one production line.

From single prototypes to volume production, you work with one point of contact throughout.
