What We Can Customize

We work with customers to manufacture optical components that match their specific application requirements. Below are the key parameters we customize.

Optical Components

Lenses, windows, mirrors, prisms, filters, beamsplitters, waveplates and polarizers. Standard or custom geometries.

Materials

BK7, fused silica, CaF₂, sapphire, ZnSe, germanium, silicon and other optical materials. We help select the right material for your wavelength and environment.

Coatings

Anti-reflection, high-reflectance, bandpass, dichroic, metallic and custom coating designs. Optimized for your wavelength and power requirements.

Specifications

Dimensions, tolerances, surface quality, surface flatness, scratch-dig and other parameters. We help balance performance and cost.

Assemblies

Multi-element optical assemblies, lens mounts, and integrated optical modules. From single components to assembled systems.

Quantities

From single prototypes to production runs. We support prototype development, small batches and volume manufacturing.

How Custom Manufacturing Works

We coordinate the key stages from your initial requirement to the finished optical component.

1

Send Your Requirement

Drawing, specification sheet, or simply describe your application and requirements.

2

Engineering Review

We review material, coating, tolerance and manufacturing feasibility. We may suggest optimizations.

3

Quotation

Clear pricing with lead time. We identify the most suitable manufacturing route for your project.

4

Manufacture & Inspect

Precision manufacturing, coating and inspection according to agreed specifications.

5

Delivery

Packed and shipped with inspection documentation when required. One point of contact throughout.

What to Send Us

Complete Specification

  • Optical drawing or detailed specification
  • Material and coating requirements
  • Tolerances and surface quality
  • Quantity and target price

Application Description

  • What is the optic used for?
  • Wavelength and power level
  • Operating environment
  • Key performance requirements
