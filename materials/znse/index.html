Zinc Selenide (ZnSe)

Zinc Selenide (ZnSe) is a polycrystalline infrared material with excellent transmission from the visible through the far infrared. It is the standard material for CO₂ laser optics and is widely used in thermal imaging and IR spectroscopy systems.

Key Optical Properties

Property Value
Transmission Range 0.6 μm – 18 μm
Refractive Index 2.4028 @ 10.6μm
Thermal Expansion 7.1 × 10⁻⁶ /K (20-300°C)
Density 5.27 g/cm³
Knoop Hardness 120 HK
✓

Advantages

  • Excellent IR transmission (0.6μm to 18μm)
  • Good thermal conductivity for CO₂ lasers
  • Standard material for 10.6μm CO₂ optics
  • Visible and IR transmission — useful for alignment
  • Low absorption at CO₂ wavelength
⚠

Limitations

  • Soft material — easily scratched
  • Toxic material — special handling required
  • High refractive index requires AR coating
  • More expensive than Ge or Si
  • Yellow/orange color (visible light absorption)

Typical Applications

CO₂ laser windows and lenses

Thermal imaging systems

FTIR spectroscopy

Medical laser systems

IR optical sensors
