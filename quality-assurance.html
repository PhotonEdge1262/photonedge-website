Inspection Capabilities

Our metrology lab is equipped with industry-leading instruments for comprehensive optical testing

🔍

Laser Interferometry

Surface figure measurement to lambda/10 (PV) and lambda/20 (RMS) using Zygo and NewView interferometers. Accurate radius, irregularity, and wavefront error characterization.

📊

Spectrophotometry

Full spectral characterization from 190nm to 3300nm. Transmittance, reflectance, and absorption measurements with 0.1% accuracy for coating verification.

📐

Dimensional Inspection

Precision measurement of diameter, thickness, wedge angle, and centering with accuracy down to +/-0.01mm using calibrated micrometers, calipers, and CMM equipment.

✨

Surface Quality (Scratch-Dig)

Visual inspection per MIL-PRF-13830B and ISO 10110-7 standards. Inspection under controlled lighting with calibrated reference standards from 10-5 to 80-50.

💡

Laser Damage Threshold (LIDT) Testing

1-on-1 and S-on-1 laser damage testing per ISO 21254. Characterization of coating durability for high-power laser applications from UV to IR wavelengths.

🌡

Environmental Testing

Temperature cycling, humidity resistance, and adhesion testing per MIL-HDBK-348. Ensures coating reliability in harsh operating environments from -40C to +250C.

Documentation & Traceability

Every shipment includes comprehensive quality documentation for full traceability

📄

Inspection Reports

Detailed measurement data for every component: surface figure, surface quality, dimensional specs, coating performance. Available as PDF with every shipment.

📋

Material Certificates

Material batch certificates with full traceability to raw material supplier. Includes refractive index, homogeneity grade, stress birefringence, and environmental classification.

🔗

Coating Documentation

Coating design reports with measured spectral curves, environmental test results, and LIDT data. Laser coating documentation per ISO 11254 for high-power applications.

📦

Certificate of Conformity

CoC with every order confirming all specifications have been met. Traceable to calibration standards. Available in English and Chinese.
