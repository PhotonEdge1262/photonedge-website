Quality Assurance

Quality Verification Process

How do we verify your optic? A structured inspection process from incoming material to final shipment.

Inspection & Verification Flow

Every optical component goes through a structured verification process. Here is how we ensure your specification is met at every stage.

1

Incoming Material Inspection

Raw optical materials are verified against specification before release to production.

2

In-Process Inspection

Critical dimensions and surface quality are checked during manufacturing stages.

3

Optical Inspection

Transmission, reflectance and wavefront performance verified per agreed specifications.

4

Dimensional Inspection

Final mechanical dimensions checked using calibrated instruments.

5

Coating Verification

Coating performance verified against specified requirements.

6

Final Inspection

Complete visual, dimensional and optical inspection before packaging.

7

Documentation

Inspection reports, material certificates and conformity documents prepared per project requirements.

✓

Shipment

Components packaged according to optical handling requirements with complete documentation.
