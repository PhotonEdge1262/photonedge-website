QUALITY ASSURANCE

Quality Is Not a Claim.
It Is a Measurable Process.

Every optical component we deliver goes through a documented inspection process. Here is how we ensure consistency, accuracy, and reliability — from incoming material to final packaging.

Our Quality System

We follow a five-stage inspection framework that covers the entire production lifecycle. Each stage has defined acceptance criteria, responsible personnel, and documentation requirements.

01

Incoming Material Inspection

All raw materials and blanks are inspected before entering production. This stage prevents defective materials from affecting downstream processes.

What We Check
  • Material certificate verification (refractive index, homogeneity class)
  • Visual inspection for scratches, digs, bubbles, inclusions
  • Dimensional check (diameter, thickness, wedge)
  • Surface flatness verification on reference flat
Equipment
  • Calibrated micrometers and calipers (±0.01mm)
  • Optical flat + monochromatic light for flatness
  • Magnification lamp (10x) for surface defects
  • Refractometer for material verification
02

In-Process Inspection

Critical parameters are verified at each manufacturing stage — grinding, fine grinding, polishing, edging, and coating. This catches issues early when they are cheapest to fix.

Process Checkpoints
  • After grinding: radius/dimension within process tolerance
  • After fine grinding: surface preparation for polishing
  • After polishing: surface quality, irregularity, centering
  • After coating: optical performance (transmission/reflectance)
Key Metrics
  • Surface irregularity (power/irregularity per drawing)
  • Centering error (arcminutes)
  • Surface quality (scratch-dig per MIL-PRF-13830B)
  • Coating spectral performance (spectrophotometer)
03

04

Packaging & Cleanliness

Optical components are cleaned and packaged in a controlled environment to prevent contamination during transit. Packaging is designed to protect against mechanical shock, moisture, and electrostatic discharge.

  • Cleaned with electronic-grade IPA in lint-free environment
  • Individually wrapped in cleanroom-grade tissue
  • Placed in foam-cut trays or protective tubes
  • Outer box with shock-absorbing materials
  • Desiccant packs for moisture-sensitive materials (CaF2, ZnSe)
  • Anti-static packaging for coated optics
05

Documentation & Traceability

Every shipment includes complete documentation. We maintain full traceability from raw material lot through final inspection, enabling us to trace any optic back to its source material and processing history.

Standard Documentation
  • Inspection report (dimensional + surface quality)
  • Material certificate
  • Coating specification compliance sheet
  • Packing list with part numbers and quantities
Available Upon Request
  • Interferometry data (if applicable)
  • Spectrophotometry curves
  • Laser damage threshold test data
  • Environmental test reports (humidity, temperature cycling)
