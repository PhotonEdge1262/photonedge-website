Confidence Gate

Every AI recommendation comes with a confidence level. This tells you how certain the answer is — and what to do next.

LEVEL 1 VERIFIED

Answer comes directly from published datasheets, material specifications, or verified test results. High reliability.

LEVEL 2 REFERENCE

Based on standard industry practice, application notes, and typical performance ranges. Good for design exploration.

LEVEL 3 PROJECT-SPECIFIC

Depends on your exact specifications and operating conditions. Our engineers will validate for your project.

LEVEL 4 ENGINEERING REVIEW

Requires formal engineering review. Feasibility, tolerances, and manufacturing constraints need confirmation. RFQ recommended.

Important: AI recommendations are for design guidance only and do not constitute guaranteed specifications. All critical parameters must be verified through our formal quotation and engineering review process.
