What Are Dove Prisms?

Dove prisms are truncated rhomboid prisms used for image rotation in optical systems. Named after their inventor H. Dove (1899), these prisms rotate an image about its optical axis without lateral displacement when rotated about the optical axis.

Unlike other image-rotating prisms, the Dove prism operates with a single internal reflection. When the prism rotates by angle θ, the image rotates by 2θ, providing precise angular control. This makes them essential in optical scanning, target simulation, beam steering, and precision alignment systems.

When Should You Use a Dove Prism?

✔ Good Choice When

  • ✓ Continuous image rotation without lateral shift
  • ✓ Optical scanning and target simulation systems
  • ✓ Beam steering and orientation adjustment
  • ✓ Compact optical systems requiring precise angular control

⚠ Consider an Alternative When

✕ 90° image rotation needed Penta Prism
✕ Image inversion without rotation Porro Prism
✕ Perpendicular beam deviation Right-Angle Prism
✕ Wide-field beam displacement Parallel Flat

Engineering Notes

Image Rotation Ratio

Dove prism rotates image by 2θ when prism rotates by θ. This 2:1 ratio must be accounted for in scanning system calibration.

Beam Diameter Limitation

The usable aperture is limited by the prism face width. Ensure beam diameter does not exceed ~60% of the prism face to avoid vignetting.

Converging Beam Warning

Dove prisms introduce astigmatism in converging beams. Use in collimated space or add compensating optics for focused systems.

Mounting Considerations

Mount in a precision rotation stage with the optical axis aligned through the prism center. Kinematic mounts recommended for scanning applications.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does a Dove prism rotate an image?

When a Dove prism is rotated about its optical axis by angle θ, the internal reflection causes the image to rotate by 2θ. This provides precise, continuous image rotation without lateral beam displacement.

What materials are available?

Standard materials include N-BK7 for visible applications, UV Fused Silica for UV-VIS-NIR, and CaF₂ for broadband UV-IR. Custom materials available upon request.

Can Dove prisms be used with converging beams?

Dove prisms introduce astigmatism in converging beams. They work best in collimated optical paths. If converging beam use is required, consult our engineering team for compensation options.

What is the maximum beam diameter?

The beam should not exceed approximately 60% of the prism face width to avoid vignetting at the internal reflection surface. Specify your beam diameter for our recommendation.

What surface quality do you offer?

60-40 for standard applications, 40-20 for precision, and 20-10 for laser-grade optics. Surface quality directly affects scatter and throughput.

What is the lead time?

Standard Dove prisms ship within 1-2 weeks. Custom sizes, materials, or coatings require 3-4 weeks.
