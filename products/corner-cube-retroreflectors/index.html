What Is a Corner Cube Retroreflectors?

Corner cube retroreflectors (also called retroreflecting prisms or corner cubes) consist of three mutually perpendicular reflecting surfaces that return an incident beam parallel to its input direction, regardless of the angle of incidence (within the acceptance angle).

This unique property makes them essential for distance measurement (interferometry, LIDAR), alignment systems, optical surveying, and laser ranging applications.

✗ 90° beam deviation → Right Angle Prism
✗ Image rotation → Dove Prism
✗ Constant deviation → Penta Prism
✗ Retroreflection at low cost → Hollow retroreflector

Optical Coating Options

AR Coated

AR on entrance face. TIR on internal surfaces.

Typical use: General retroreflection

Metal-Coated

Enhanced aluminum or silver on reflecting surfaces.

Typical use: Extended acceptance angle, non-TIR designs

HR Coated

Dielectric HR for laser applications.

Typical use: High-power laser retroreflection

How to Specify Your Coating

To select the right coating, please provide: (1) Operating wavelength, (2) Angle of incidence, (3) Required transmission or reflectivity, (4) Laser power density (if applicable), (5) Operating environment.

Engineering Considerations

Key factors to evaluate when specifying this optical component for your system.

Acceptance Angle

Limited by aperture and geometry. Larger apertures accept wider angle ranges.

Wavefront Error

λ/4 is standard. For interferometric applications, request λ/10 or tighter.

TIR vs Metal-Coated

Total internal reflection (TIR) is polarization-dependent at wide angles. Metal coatings reduce this effect.

Retroreflection Accuracy

Internal angle accuracy determines beam deviation from perfect retroreflection. Specify ±2 arc sec for precision use.

Frequently Asked Questions

What materials are available?

Standard materials include N-BK7 for visible applications, UV Fused Silica for UV, and CaF₂ for broadband UV-IR.

What are the standard sizes?

We stock common sizes from 5mm to 50mm. Custom dimensions are available upon request.

What surface quality do you offer?

60-40 for standard applications, 40-20 for precision, and 20-10 for laser-grade optics.

Can prisms be used with lasers?

Yes. For laser applications, specify laser-grade surface quality (20-10) and appropriate AR coatings for your wavelength.

How do I specify a prism?

Provide: type, material, dimensions, wavelength, surface quality, and coating requirements.

What is the lead time?

Standard prisms ship within 1-2 weeks. Custom prisms require 3-4 weeks.

What is a corner cube retroreflector?

A prism with three mutually perpendicular surfaces that returns an incident beam parallel to its input, regardless of angle (within acceptance angle).

What is the acceptance angle?

The maximum input angle for which the beam is properly retroreflected. Depends on aperture and geometry.

TIR or metal-coated?

TIR is standard and lossless but polarization-dependent at wide angles. Metal coatings work at wider angles but add loss.
