Material Options

Select the optimal optical material based on your wavelength, power, thermal environment and mechanical requirements.

  • N-BK7 (K9) — Most economical substrate for visible-NIR flat mirrors. Good polishability and optical homogeneity.
  • UV Fused Silica — For UV applications or high-power laser systems requiring higher LIDT and lower thermal expansion.
  • Zerodur / Pyrex — Ultra-low CTE substrates for applications requiring dimensional stability across temperature changes.

Coating Options

Coatings are engineered around your specific wavelength and operating conditions.

  • Enhanced Aluminum — R > 92% from 200nm to 20um. Broadest broadband coverage. Standard for general-purpose beam steering.
  • Protected Silver — R > 98% from 400nm to 20um. Higher reflectance than Al for visible-IR systems. Better environmental stability than bare silver.
  • Protected Gold — R > 96% from 600nm to 20um. Preferred for IR and thermal imaging applications.
  • Dielectric HR (Custom) — R > 99.5% at specific wavelengths (266nm, 355nm, 532nm, 1064nm, etc.). High LIDT for laser systems.

Engineering Considerations

Coating selection: Enhanced aluminum offers the broadest wavelength coverage but lower reflectance (~92%). Protected silver gives higher reflectance (~98%) in visible-IR. Dielectric HR provides the highest reflectance (>99.5%) but only at specific wavelengths. Match coating to your wavelength and reflectance requirements.

Flatness requirements: For beam steering where wavefront quality is not critical, lambda/4 is sufficient. For interferometry or imaging applications, specify lambda/10 or better to avoid wavefront distortion.

Front surface vs. rear surface: All precision optical mirrors are front-surface (first-surface) mirrors where reflection occurs at the coated surface. Rear-surface mirrors (like household mirrors) introduce ghost reflections and are not suitable for optical systems.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between enhanced aluminum and protected silver mirrors?
Enhanced aluminum offers broader wavelength coverage (200nm-20um) with ~92% reflectance. Protected silver offers higher reflectance (~98%) but only from ~400nm onward. Choose aluminum for UV-visible-IR broadband needs, or silver when you need higher reflectance in visible-IR.
Can I use BK7 mirrors for high-power laser applications?
BK7 mirrors with dielectric HR coating can handle moderate laser powers. For high-power (>100W CW) applications, consider fused silica substrates with ion-beam sputtered (IBS) dielectric coatings for higher laser-induced damage threshold (LIDT).
