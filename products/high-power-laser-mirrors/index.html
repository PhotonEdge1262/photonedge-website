High-Power Laser Mirrors

Material Options

Select the optimal optical material based on your wavelength, power, thermal environment and mechanical requirements.

  • UV Fused Silica — Low absorption, high LIDT substrate. Standard for >100W CW and pulsed laser systems.
  • N-BK7 — Cost-effective for moderate power visible-NIR applications below 50W CW.
  • Silicon / Cu (Metallic) — For CO2 laser (10.6um) high-power systems. Metallic substrates with enhanced cooling.

Coating Options

Coatings are engineered around your specific wavelength and operating conditions.

  • Dielectric HR @ 1064nm — R > 99.99%. Ion-beam sputtered for maximum LIDT. For Nd:YAG and fiber laser resonators.
  • Dielectric HR @ 532nm — R > 99.9%. For frequency-doubled Nd:YAG/Green laser systems.
  • Dielectric HR @ 355nm — R > 99.5%. UV-optimized for Nd:YAG 3rd harmonic. Ion-beam sputtered.
  • Protected Silver (Broadband) — R > 98% from 400nm-20um. For broadband or multi-wavelength beam steering.

Engineering Considerations

LIDT specification: Always provide your complete laser parameters: wavelength, power/energy, pulse duration, repetition rate, beam diameter, and polarization. LIDT varies dramatically with these parameters. A coating rated for CW may fail at the same average power in pulsed mode.

Contamination is the #1 cause of damage: Even the highest-LIDT coating will fail if contaminated with particulates, fingerprints, or organic residue. Implement clean handling procedures and regular inspection in your laser system.

Ion-beam sputtered (IBS) vs e-beam: IBS coatings offer 2-5x higher LIDT than conventional e-beam coatings at the cost of slightly higher price. For >1kW CW or >1J pulsed systems, IBS is strongly recommended.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum laser power your mirrors can handle?
With proper substrate material (fused silica) and IBS coating, our dielectric HR mirrors can handle >10kW CW at 1064nm with appropriate beam diameters. For specific power levels, contact us with your complete laser parameters for coating optimization.
How do I extend the lifetime of high-power laser mirrors?
Keep optics clean. Operate at <50% of rated LIDT. Ensure beam diameter is large enough to keep fluence well below damage threshold. Inspect regularly for contamination. Store in clean, dry conditions when not in use.
What coating process gives the highest laser damage threshold?
Ion-beam sputtered (IBS) coatings consistently achieve the highest LIDT due to their dense, low-absorption microstructure. For UV wavelengths, IBS is essential. E-beam coatings are adequate for moderate power visible-NIR applications.
