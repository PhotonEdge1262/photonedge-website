What Are BK7 Optical Prisms?

BK7 optical prisms are refractive and reflective optical components fabricated from N-BK7 glass, used to deviate, disperse, redirect, or rotate light beams and images. BK7 is the most common optical glass for visible-wavelength prism applications, offering excellent homogeneity and ease of polishing.

When Should You Use This Product?

Use BK7 prisms when you need beam deviation, image erection, dispersion, or optical path folding in visible-wavelength systems. Common applications include periscopes, spectrometers, laser beam steering, rangefinders, and interferometry. For UV or high-power laser applications, consider UV fused silica prisms instead.

Optical Coating Options

Anti-Reflection (AR)

Single-layer or multi-layer AR coatings for specific wavelength bands. Reduce reflection to below 0.5% per surface.

High-Reflection (HR)

Dielectric HR coatings for prism reflection faces. Reflectivity >99.5% at design wavelength.

Broadband AR

BBAR coatings for wide spectral range applications. Low reflection across 400-700nm or other bands.

Laser Line Coatings

Optimized for specific laser wavelengths: 266nm, 355nm, 532nm, 1064nm, 10.6µm, etc.

Custom Coating

Custom coating designs for unique wavelength ranges, angles of incidence, and polarization requirements.

Engineering Considerations

Prism Type Selection

Choose the prism geometry based on your optical function: beam deviation (right angle), image rotation (dove), constant deviation (penta), dispersion (equilateral), or retroreflection (corner cube).

Angle Tolerance

Prism angle accuracy directly affects beam pointing. Standard ±1 arc-minute is suitable for most applications; precision ±30 arc-second for interferometry and metrology.

Wavelength Range

BK7 transmits well from ~350nm to ~2µm. For deep UV below 350nm, use UV fused silica. For mid-IR beyond 2µm, consider CaF₂ or other IR materials.

Surface Quality

Tighter scratch-dig specifications increase cost. 60-40 is adequate for most alignment and beam-steering applications; 20-10 for imaging and laser systems.

Applications

Laser Systems

Beam steering, folding, and dispersion in laser cavities and delivery systems.

Spectroscopy

Dispersing prisms for wavelength separation in spectrometers and monochromators.

Metrology & Interferometry

Precision prisms for coordinate measurement, interferometers, and alignment systems.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are BK7 optical prisms used for?

BK7 Optical Prisms are used in optical systems where precise control of light is required. Common applications include laser systems, imaging, spectroscopy, and scientific instrumentation.

What is the wavelength range?

BK7 transmits from approximately 350nm to 2µm. The usable range depends on the specific prism type and any applied coatings. For UV applications below 350nm, consider UV fused silica prisms.

Can I order custom sizes or specifications?

Yes. PhotonEdge offers custom diameters, focal lengths, and coating specifications outside our standard catalog. Custom optics require engineering evaluation. Lead time is confirmed with your quotation.
