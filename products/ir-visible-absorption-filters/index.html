About This Product

IR transmission visible absorption filters transmit infrared light while absorbing visible wavelengths. Made from red glass types (HB600-HB720), they are widely used in IR imaging, night vision systems, and spectral separation applications.

How to Specify Your Coating

To select the right coating, please provide: (1) Operating wavelength, (2) Angle of incidence, (3) Required transmission or reflectivity, (4) Laser power density (if applicable), (5) Operating environment.

Engineering Considerations

Key factors to evaluate when specifying this optical component for your system.

Wavelength

Ensure the material and coating are optimized for your operating wavelength range: NIR, SWIR, MWIR, LWIR.

Surface Quality

40-20 is suitable for most applications. For precision imaging or scattering-sensitive systems, specify 20-10 or better.

Environment

Consider temperature, humidity, and mechanical stress when selecting material and coating specifications.

Application
