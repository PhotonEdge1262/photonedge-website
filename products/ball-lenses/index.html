What Is a Ball Lenses?

Ball lenses are perfect spheres of optical glass or crystal that function as short focal length lenses. Their symmetric geometry provides self-centering in mechanical housings, making them ideal for fiber optic coupling and miniature optical assemblies.

Commonly used in fiber optic communication, endoscopy, sensor systems, and any application requiring miniature focusing or collimation in a compact package.

When Should You Use This Product?

✔ Good Choice When

  • ✓ Fiber-to-fiber or fiber-to-detector coupling
  • ✓ Miniature optical systems with tight space constraints
  • ✓ Endoscopy and medical imaging probes
  • ✓ Sensor systems requiring compact focusing

⚠ Consider an Alternative When

✗ Larger aperture focusing needed → Plano-Convex Lens
✗ High-quality imaging → Aspheric Lens
✗ Rod-shaped illumination → Rod Lens
✗ Collimating laser diodes → C-Mount Lens

Optical Coating Options

AR 350-650nm

Broadband AR for visible range. R < 0.5% per surface.

Typical use: General visible laser and imaging

AR 650-950nm

AR for NIR range (780nm, 808nm, 850nm diodes).

Typical use: NIR laser diode collimation and fiber coupling

AR 950-1250nm

AR for 1064nm and nearby wavelengths.

Typical use: Nd:YAG and fiber laser systems

BBAR

Low reflection across 400-1000nm broadband.

Typical use: Multispectral applications

Uncoated

Raw glass surface. ~4% reflection per surface.

Typical use: Non-critical applications

How to Specify Your Coating

To select the right coating, please provide: (1) Operating wavelength, (2) Angle of incidence, (3) Required transmission or reflectivity, (4) Laser power density (if applicable), (5) Operating environment.

Engineering Considerations

Key factors to evaluate when specifying this optical component for your system.

Diameter and EFL

The effective focal length of a ball lens is approximately f ≈ n·D/(4(n-1)), where n is refractive index and D is diameter.

Working Distance

Very short working distance due to short focal length. Plan coupling geometry carefully.

Self-Centering

Spherical shape allows automatic centering in precision bores, simplifying mechanical design.

AR Coatings

With two spherical surfaces, AR coatings significantly improve throughput for multi-surface systems.

Frequently Asked Questions

What materials are available for this lens type?

We offer multiple substrate materials including N-BK7, UV Fused Silica, CaF₂, and Sapphire depending on your wavelength and environmental requirements.

Can I order custom specifications?

Yes. PhotonEdge offers custom diameters, focal lengths, coatings, and surface qualities. Custom specifications typically require 4-6 weeks lead time.

What surface quality should I specify?

40-20 is suitable for most laser and imaging applications. 20-10 is recommended for precision systems where scattering must be minimized.

What coating do I need?

The coating depends on your operating wavelength. For visible applications, AR 350-650nm is standard. For NIR lasers, choose the coating matching your wavelength. Uncoated is suitable for non-critical applications.

Yes. Dimensional reports, surface quality verification, and coating performance data are available according to project requirements.
