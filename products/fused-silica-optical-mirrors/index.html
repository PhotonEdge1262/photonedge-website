Fused Silica Optical Mirrors

High-flatness UV fused silica mirror substrates with precision dielectric and metallic coatings for laser beam steering, interferometry, and high-power laser systems. Thermal stability ensures consistent performance under varying conditions.

Material Options

Select the optimal optical material based on your wavelength, power, thermal environment and mechanical requirements.

  • UV Fused Silica — Standard substrate for laser mirrors. Near-zero CTE, high LIDT, excellent UV to NIR transmission before coating.
  • N-BK7 — Economical substrate for visible-NIR mirrors where thermal stability is less critical.
  • Zerodur / ULE — Ultra-low expansion substrates for interferometry-grade mirrors where thermal stability is paramount.

Coating Options

Coatings are engineered around your specific wavelength and operating conditions.

  • Broadband Dielectric HR — > 99.9% reflectance across specified band. High LIDT. Custom wavelength bands available.
  • Protected Silver — > 98% reflectance from 400nm-20um. Broadband solution for multi-wavelength systems.
  • Protected Gold — > 98% reflectance from 600nm-20um. Standard for IR and thermal imaging applications.
  • Laser-line HR — Peak reflectance > 99.99% at specific wavelength. Optimized for maximum LIDT.

Manufacturing Tolerances

We optimize specifications to match your system requirements — not over-specify. Tighter tolerances increase cost without always improving system performance. Typical manufacturing capabilities:

  • Surface quality: from 60-40 to 10-5 scratch-dig
  • Surface flatness: from λ/4 to λ/20 per inch
  • Dimensional tolerance: ±0.01mm to ±0.05mm
  • Centration: <3 arc min standard, <30 arc sec precision

Engineering Considerations

Flatness selection: For beam steering in most laser systems, lambda/10 is sufficient. For interferometry reference surfaces or cavity mirrors, specify lambda/20 or lambda/50. Tighter flatness increases cost substantially.

Coating LIDT: For high-power lasers, dielectric HR coatings offer the highest damage thresholds (>20 J/cm2). Protected metal coatings have lower LIDT but broader spectral range. Always specify your laser parameters.

Thermal considerations: Fused silica substrates minimize thermal distortion at high average powers. For >100W CW lasers, fused silica is strongly preferred over BK7.

Frequently Asked Questions

What flatness do I need for my laser mirror?
For general beam steering: lambda/4 to lambda/10. For precision interferometry: lambda/20 to lambda/50. For resonator cavities: lambda/10 minimum. Tighter flatness significantly increases cost, so specify only what your system requires.
Can you provide custom-sized mirrors?
Yes. We manufacture fused silica mirrors in custom sizes from 6mm to 300mm diameter. Non-standard thicknesses, edge profiles (beveled, flat), and mounting features are all available.
