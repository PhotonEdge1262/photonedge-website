What Is a Laser Line Filters?

Laser Line Filters are precision optical filters designed for laser line isolation and cleanup. They use thin-film interference to selectively transmit or block specific wavelength bands.

PhotonEdge designs custom filter coatings optimized for your specific wavelength requirements and application environment.

When Should You Use This Product?

✔ Good Choice When

  • ✓ Laser line isolation and cleanup
  • ✓ Fluorescence signal extraction
  • ✓ Raman spectroscopy
  • ✓ Machine vision with laser illumination

⚠ Consider an Alternative When

✗ Broader bandpass needed → Narrow Band Filter
✗ Multiple wavelengths → Dichroic Filter
✗ Variable attenuation → Neutral Density Filter
✗ Edge filtering → Longpass/Shortpass Filter

How to Specify Your Coating

To select the right coating, please provide: (1) Operating wavelength, (2) Angle of incidence, (3) Required transmission or reflectivity, (4) Laser power density (if applicable), (5) Operating environment.

Engineering Considerations

Key factors to evaluate when specifying this optical component for your system.

Center Wavelength / Cut-off

Specify your target wavelength. For filters at non-normal incidence, account for AOI-induced wavelength shift.

Bandwidth / Edge Steepness

Narrower bandwidth or steeper edges increase cost. Specify the minimum performance required.

Blocking (OD)

Higher OD provides better out-of-band rejection but may reduce peak transmission.

AOI Effects

Increasing AOI shifts transmission curves toward shorter wavelengths. Specify your beam geometry for accurate performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I specify custom wavelengths?

Yes. We design custom filters for any center wavelength from 200nm to 2500nm. Provide your target specifications for a custom design.

How does angle of incidence affect filter performance?

For bandpass filters, increasing AOI shifts the center wavelength toward shorter wavelengths (blue shift). Always specify your beam geometry.

What does OD 4 blocking mean?

OD 4 means the filter blocks 99.99% of out-of-band light (transmission < 0.01%). Higher OD provides better blocking but may reduce peak transmission.

What substrate should I choose?

BK7 for visible, UV Fused Silica for UV wavelengths, Borosilicate for economy visible-only applications.

Can these filters handle high-power laser?

Hard dielectric coatings handle moderate to high powers. Specify laser power density for proper coating selection.

What is the typical lead time?

Standard filters ship within 1-2 weeks. Custom designs require 3-4 weeks.

What is a laser line filter?

A narrow bandpass filter designed to transmit only the laser wavelength while blocking pump light, fluorescence, and ambient light.

How narrow can the bandwidth be?

Bandwidths from 1nm to 10nm are typical. Narrower bandwidths provide better isolation but lower peak transmission.
