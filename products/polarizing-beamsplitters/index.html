What Is a Polarizing Beamsplitters?

Polarizing Beamsplitters divide an incident beam into two paths with controlled splitting ratios. Polarization-dependent beamsplitters separating S and P polarization.

PhotonEdge beamsplitters are manufactured with precision substrates and controlled coating processes for reliable, repeatable performance across your optical system.

When Should You Use This Product?

✔ Good Choice When

  • ✓ Laser polarization separation
  • ✓ Polarization-based optical isolation
  • ✓ Ellipsometry and polarization measurements
  • ✓ Optical system polarization management

⚠ Consider an Alternative When

✗ Equal split, no polarization → Non-Polarizing Beamsplitter
✗ Higher extinction → Glan-Thompson Prism
✗ Plate form factor → Plate Beamsplitter
✗ Waveplate combination → Waveplates + Polarizer

How to Specify Your Coating

To select the right coating, please provide: (1) Operating wavelength, (2) Angle of incidence, (3) Required transmission or reflectivity, (4) Laser power density (if applicable), (5) Operating environment.

Engineering Considerations

Key factors to evaluate when specifying this optical component for your system.

Splitting Ratio

Specify required T/R ratio and tolerance. Standard 50/50 ±5%. Custom ratios available.

Wavelength Dependence

Dielectric coatings are wavelength-specific. Metallic coatings are more broadband.

Polarization Effects

Standard cubes are polarization-dependent. Specify NPBS for polarization-independent splitting.

Ghost Reflections

Plate beamsplitters produce ghost reflections from the uncoated surface. Use wedge plates or cubes to eliminate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What splitting ratios are available?

Standard ratios include 50/50, 70/30, 90/10, and custom ratios. Both transmitting and reflecting percentages can be specified.

What is the difference between cube and plate beamsplitters?

Cube beamsplitters use cemented prisms with coating at the diagonal. Plate beamsplitters use a single substrate with coating on one surface. Cubes are more compact; plates offer more design flexibility.

Can I specify polarization-dependent or non-polarizing?

Yes. Both polarizing (PBS) and non-polarizing (NPBS) beamsplitters are available.

What wavelength range do you support?

From deep UV (200nm) through visible to mid-IR (5µm+), depending on substrate and coating materials.

How do I specify a beamsplitter?

Provide: type (cube/plate), splitting ratio, wavelength, polarization requirement, size, and coating.

What is the lead time?

Standard beamsplitters ship within 1-2 weeks. Custom designs require 3-4 weeks.
