What Is a Protected Aluminum Mirrors?

Protected Aluminum Mirrors provide high-reflectivity optical surfaces for laser beam steering, alignment, and general optical systems. PhotonEdge mirrors are manufactured with precision substrates and controlled coating processes to ensure reliable performance.

When Should You Use This Product?

✔ Good Choice When

  • ✓ UV to NIR broadband applications
  • ✓ General-purpose laboratory mirrors
  • ✓ Alignment and beam steering
  • ✓ Budget broadband reflection

⚠ Consider an Alternative When

✗ Higher reflectance VIS-NIR → Protected Silver Mirror
✗ Wavelength-specific HR → Dielectric Mirror
✗ IR broadband (> 700nm) → Protected Gold Mirror
✗ Enhanced visible → Enhanced Aluminum Mirror

How to Specify Your Coating

To select the right coating, please provide: (1) Operating wavelength, (2) Angle of incidence, (3) Required transmission or reflectivity, (4) Laser power density (if applicable), (5) Operating environment.

Engineering Considerations

Key factors to evaluate when specifying this optical component for your system.

Reflectivity

Verify reflectance meets your system throughput requirements. Factor in both reflection losses and absorption.

AOI

Specify angle of incidence for proper coating design. Non-normal AOI causes S/P polarization differences.

LIDT

For laser applications, specify power density and pulse parameters to ensure coating durability.

Handling

Handle mirrors by edges only. Clean with compressed gas first, then optical-grade solvents if needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What reflectivity can I expect?

Reflectivity depends on the coating: Protected Silver > 97% (400-2000nm), Protected Al > 90% (250-2000nm), Dielectric HR > 99.9% at design wavelength.

What is the laser damage threshold?

LIDT depends on coating type. Dielectric HR coatings typically have the highest LIDT. Specify your laser parameters for proper coating selection.

What substrates are available?

Standard substrates include BK7, UV Fused Silica, and Float Glass. For IR applications, we offer Cu, Mo, and Si substrates.

Can mirrors be used at non-normal incidence?

Yes. Specify the angle of incidence (AOI) for proper coating design. S-polarization and P-polarization have different reflectance at oblique angles.

How should I handle and clean mirrors?

Handle by edges only. Use compressed gas for dust removal. For smudges, use optical-grade cleaning solvents with lint-free wipes. Avoid touching coated surfaces.

What is the lead time for custom mirrors?

Standard catalog mirrors ship within 1-2 weeks, depending on quantity and specifications. Custom-coated mirrors typically require 3-4 weeks, subject to change based on order specifics.

What is the UV advantage over silver?

Aluminum reflects well into the UV (~250nm), while silver reflectance drops below ~400nm.
