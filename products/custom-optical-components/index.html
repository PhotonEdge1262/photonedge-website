Material Options

Select the optimal optical material based on your wavelength, power, thermal environment and mechanical requirements.

  • BK7 / N-BK7 — Most common optical glass. Visible-NIR, cost-effective.
  • UV Fused Silica — UV to NIR, high LIDT, near-zero thermal expansion.
  • CaF₂ — Broadest UV-IR transmission, low birefringence.
  • ZnSe — CO2 laser and IR, 0.6–20µm.
  • Germanium & Silicon — Thermal imaging and MWIR/LWIR.
  • Sapphire — Extreme hardness, high temperature, 150–5500nm.
  • Specialty glasses — SF11, LaK, LaSF and other optical glasses available on request.

Coating Options

Coatings are engineered around your specific wavelength and operating conditions.

  • Anti-Reflection (AR) — Single-band, dual-band or broadband. Minimize reflection loss at your wavelength.
  • High Reflectance (HR) — Dielectric HR mirrors with R>99.9% at design wavelength.
  • Broadband Dielectric — BBAR or BB HR for multi-wavelength systems.
  • Dichroic — Custom wavelength separation for fluorescence, beam combining.
  • Metallic — Al, Ag, Au coatings for broadband reflection.
  • Custom designs — Send us your spectral requirements — we design and verify the coating.

Manufacturing Tolerances

We optimize specifications to match your system requirements — not over-specify. Tighter tolerances increase cost without always improving system performance. Our engineers will work with you to identify which parameters actually matter for your application and where standard tolerances are sufficient. Typical manufacturing capabilities:

  • Surface quality: from 60-40 to 10-5 scratch-dig
  • Surface flatness: from λ/4 to λ/20 per inch
  • Dimensional tolerance: ±0.01mm to ±0.05mm
  • Centration: <3 arc min standard, <30 arc sec precision

Inspection & Quality

Every component undergoes 100% inspection before shipment. We provide full inspection documentation including interferometer reports, spectral transmission curves, surface quality images and dimensional measurement data. Equipment capabilities:

  • Laser interferometry: λ/100 resolution for flatness and wavefront
  • Spectrophotometry: 190nm – 25μm transmission and reflection
  • Surface inspection: MIL-PRF-13830B compliant
  • Dimensional metrology: ±0.01mm accuracy

Engineering Considerations

Design for manufacturability (DFM): Share your system requirements, not just drawings. Our engineers can often suggest specification adjustments that reduce cost without affecting performance — for example, relaxing flatness from λ/10 to λ/4 where your system allows it.

Prototype to production: We maintain the same manufacturing standards for prototypes and volume production. A prototype that works will scale to volume without surprises. Every production run includes first-article inspection matching your prototype specifications.

Drawing vs. specification: If you have a detailed drawing with all parameters specified, we can quote directly. If you have a system requirement but no optical design, our engineering team can help specify the optimal component for your application at no charge.

Frequently Asked Questions

What information do you need to provide a quote for custom optics?
At minimum: material (if known), diameter, thickness, surface quality, flatness, and coating requirements. A technical drawing is ideal. If you only have system-level requirements (wavelength, power, environment), our engineers can help specify the optical component — this engineering review is free with every quote.
What is the typical lead time for custom optical components?
Standard prototypes: 5–10 business days. Simple custom components (windows, flat mirrors): 2–3 weeks. Complex components (aspheres, multi-element assemblies): 3–6 weeks. Rush orders are available for most components — contact us with your timeline.
Can you manufacture optical assemblies, not just individual components?
Yes. We provide complete optical assemblies including lens modules, mirror mounts with installed optics, and integrated opto-mechanical sub-systems. Assembly capabilities include alignment, bonding, and integration with customer-provided mechanical housings.
Do you have minimum order quantities?
No minimum order quantity. We regularly produce single prototypes and small batches. Volume pricing applies for orders above certain quantities — ask your sales engineer for details.
