What Is a Custom Optical Components?

PhotonEdge provides custom optical component manufacturing services for applications requiring non-standard specifications. From single prototypes to volume production, we coordinate material sourcing, precision manufacturing, coating, and inspection through our optical manufacturing network.

We work with your engineering team to review specifications, optimize manufacturability, and deliver components that meet your system requirements.

Optical Coating Options

Designed for your wavelength and AOI.

Typical use: All custom components

Custom HR

Wavelength-specific high reflectance.

Typical use: Laser and mirror components

Custom Filter

Bandpass, longpass, shortpass, dichroic.

Typical use: Filter and spectral components

Metallic

Silver, aluminum, gold for broadband reflection.

Typical use: Mirror components

How to Specify Your Coating

To select the right coating, please provide: (1) Operating wavelength, (2) Angle of incidence, (3) Required transmission or reflectivity, (4) Laser power density (if applicable), (5) Operating environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit a custom request?

Upload your drawing via the RFQ form, or describe your requirements in detail. Include material, dimensions, tolerances, surface quality, coating, and quantity.

What is the typical lead time?

Simple components: 2-3 weeks. Complex designs: 4-6 weeks. Prototypes may be faster.

Can you work from a sketch?

Yes. A rough sketch with key parameters is sufficient for initial review.

Do you provide inspection reports?

Yes. Dimensional reports, surface quality data, coating curves, and Certificates of Conformance are available.

What quantities do you support?

From single prototypes to 10,000+ production runs. Pricing scales with quantity.

What materials do you work with?

BK7, Fused Silica, CaF2, Sapphire, ZnSe, Ge, Si, and specialty optical materials.
