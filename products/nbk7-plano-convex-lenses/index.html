N-BK7 Plano-Convex Lenses

N-BK7 plano-convex lenses optimized for focusing and collimating visible and near-IR light beams. The most economical choice for general-purpose imaging, beam focusing, and optical systems operating in the 350-2000nm range.

Key Specifications

MaterialN-BK7 (Nd = 1.5168, Vd = 64.2)
Transmission Range330 - 2,100 nm
Diameter Range3mm - 150mm
Focal Length Range5mm - 5,000mm
Surface Quality40-20 / 20-10 (MIL-PRF-13830B)
Surface Flatnesslambda/4 @ 632.8nm (test plate)
Centration< 3 arc min
CoatingBBAR / AR @ specific wavelength / Uncoated

Material Options

Select the optimal optical material based on your wavelength, power, thermal environment and mechanical requirements.

  • N-BK7 — Best value for visible-NIR applications. Excellent optical homogeneity, wide size range availability.
  • UV Fused Silica — For UV operation below 350nm or high-power laser systems.
  • SF11 (in Achromatic Doublets) — Pair with BK7 for achromatic correction in imaging systems.

Coating Options

Coatings are engineered around your specific wavelength and operating conditions.

  • BBAR VIS-NIR — R < 0.5% across 400-1000nm. Maximizes system throughput.
  • AR @ 532nm — R < 0.1% per surface. For green laser focusing.
  • AR @ 808/1064nm — For NIR laser diode collimation.
  • Uncoated — ~4% Fresnel loss per surface. For non-critical applications.

Manufacturing Tolerances

We optimize specifications to match your system requirements — not over-specify. Tighter tolerances increase cost without always improving system performance. Typical manufacturing capabilities:

  • Surface quality: from 60-40 to 10-5 scratch-dig
  • Surface flatness: from λ/4 to λ/20 per inch
  • Dimensional tolerance: ±0.01mm to ±0.05mm
  • Centration: <3 arc min standard, <30 arc sec precision

Inspection & Quality

Every component undergoes 100% inspection before shipment with full documentation including interferometer reports, spectral curves, surface quality images and dimensional data.

  • Laser interferometry: λ/100 resolution
  • Spectrophotometry: 190nm – 25μm
  • Surface inspection: MIL-PRF-13830B compliant
  • Dimensional metrology: ±0.01mm accuracy

Applications

  • General purpose beam focusing and collimation
  • Imaging systems and relay optics
  • Machine vision and inspection systems
  • Laser beam expansion and reduction
  • Optical sensor focusing elements
  • Educational and research laboratory optics

Engineering Considerations

Orientation: For focusing a collimated beam, place the curved surface toward the collimated beam (longer conjugate). This minimizes spherical aberration for a singlet lens.

Chromatic aberration: BK7 singlet lenses have significant chromatic aberration. If your system operates across a broad wavelength range and requires sharp focus at all wavelengths, consider achromatic doublets instead.

f-number limit: For f-numbers below f/4, spherical aberration becomes significant in singlet lenses. Consider aspheric lenses for tighter focusing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between N-BK7 and standard BK7?
N-BK7 is manufactured to tighter environmental standards (no arsenic) and is the current standard designation. Optically identical to BK7. We recommend specifying N-BK7 for new designs.
When should I upgrade from BK7 to fused silica?
When your application requires UV transmission below 350nm, operation in high-power laser systems, or thermal stability across wide temperature ranges. For standard visible-NIR systems, BK7 offers the best value.
