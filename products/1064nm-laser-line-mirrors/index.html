1064nm Laser Line Mirrors

Material Options

Select the optimal optical material based on your wavelength, power, thermal environment and mechanical requirements.

  • UV Fused Silica — Low absorption, high LIDT substrate. Standard for > 100W CW and pulsed laser resonators.
  • N-BK7 — Cost-effective for moderate power visible-NIR applications below 50W CW.
  • Silicon / Cu (Metallic) — For very high power CO2 laser systems. Not typical for 1064nm but available for multi-wavelength setups.

Coating Options

Coatings are engineered around your specific wavelength and operating conditions.

  • Dielectric HR @ 1064nm — R > 99.99%. IBS for maximum LIDT. For Nd:YAG/fiber laser resonators and cavity mirrors.
  • Dielectric HR @ 1064nm + HT @ 532nm — HR at 1064nm, high transmission at 532nm. For frequency-doubled laser output couplers.
  • Partial Reflector (OC) — R = 50-95% @ 1064nm. Laser output couplers with specific reflectance for cavity optimization.
  • Dielectric HR @ 1064nm + AR @ 808nm — For side-pumped or end-pumped laser cavities with pump input through mirror.

Engineering Considerations

Output coupler vs HR mirror: A laser cavity needs both a highly reflective mirror (HR, R > 99.9%) and an output coupler (partial reflector, R = 50-95%). The output coupler reflectance determines the laser's output power and efficiency. Specify both mirrors with matching parameters.

Pulsed vs CW optimization: Coating designs differ for pulsed and CW lasers. Pulsed mirrors optimize for peak power LIDT; CW mirrors optimize for thermal dissipation. Always specify your laser mode and parameters.

IBS is essential for high-power: Ion-beam sputtered coatings offer 2-5x higher LIDT than conventional e-beam coatings. For any system above 1W pulsed or 50W CW at 1064nm, IBS is strongly recommended.

Frequently Asked Questions

What reflectance do I need for a laser resonator mirror?
For the high-reflector (HR) end of the cavity: R > 99.9% (typically 99.95-99.99%). For the output coupler: R = 50-95% depending on gain medium, pump power and desired output. We can recommend output coupler reflectance based on your laser parameters.
Can these mirrors be used for 532nm (frequency-doubled) lasers?
Standard 1064nm HR mirrors are highly reflective at 532nm as well (dielectric stacks typically reflect a band around the design wavelength). If you need a mirror that is HR at 1064nm but transmits 532nm, specify a dichroic coating.
