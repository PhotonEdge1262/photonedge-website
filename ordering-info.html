Pricing Overview

Prices below are reference ranges. Final quotation depends on specific specifications, coatings, quantities, and delivery requirements.

Product Category Standard Range Custom Range Notes
Lenses (Singlets) $5 – $150 $20 – $500+ Per piece, depends on material, diameter, coating
Windows & Substrates $8 – $200 $25 – $600+ Per piece, depends on size, material, surface quality
Mirrors $10 – $250 $30 – $800+ Per piece, depends on coating type and substrate
Filters (Bandpass/ND) $15 – $300 $40 – $1,000+ Per piece, depends on wavelength specs and coating complexity
Prisms $12 – $200 $30 – $600+ Per piece, depends on type, material, and precision
Beam Splitters $20 – $350 $50 – $900+ Per piece, depends on ratio, wavelength, and coating
Waveplates & Polarizers $25 – $400 $60 – $1,200+ Per piece, depends on retardance accuracy and aperture
Crystal Optics (CaF₂, ZnSe) $30 – $500 $80 – $2,000+ Per piece, material cost is significant factor
